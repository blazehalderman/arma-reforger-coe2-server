# Zagoria89Vehicles

> **One-line role**: Multi-vehicle Soviet/OPFOR content pack — the umbrella mod of the Zagoria89 vehicle family.

## 1. Overview

Long-running multi-vehicle content pack (v1.0.140 — heavily iterated) by Zagoria89 adding a broad mix of Soviet-era civilian and military vehicles (UAZ jeeps, GAZ trucks, Ural-series, possibly Niva variants, etc.) usable across OPFOR, ambient/civilian, and insurgent factions. This is the "kitchen sink" Zagoria89 pack; the standalone T-55, MT-LB, BMD1/2, T80U, T-34, etc. are split into their own packs.

## 2. Functionality / Features

- Wide variety of Soviet/OPFOR ground vehicles (specifics depend on current Workshop release)
- Standalone — no SpaceCore dep (unlike the Zagoria89 vehicle-specific sub-packs)
- USSR / OPFOR / civilian skins
