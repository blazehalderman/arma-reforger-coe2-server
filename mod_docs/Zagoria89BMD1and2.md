> **One-line role**: BMD-1 and BMD-2 airborne IFV content pack (Soviet VDV airborne combat vehicle) from the Zagoria89 vehicle author.

## 1. Overview

Adds the BMD-1 and BMD-2 — Soviet airborne (VDV) tracked IFVs designed for paratrooper drops — as GM-spawnable OPFOR vehicles. Part of the prolific Zagoria89 vehicle pack family.
