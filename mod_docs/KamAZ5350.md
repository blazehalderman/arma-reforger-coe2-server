# KamAZ5350

> **One-line role**: KamAZ-5350 Mustang 6x6 Russian military truck content pack.

## 1. Overview

Adds the KamAZ-5350 "Mustang" 6x6 Russian military logistics truck — the modern OPFOR analogue to the US FMTV — as a GM-spawnable OPFOR transport / cargo platform.

## 2. Functionality / Features

- KamAZ-5350 prefab(s) — cargo, troop transport, possibly armored cab variants
- Optional roof-mounted DShK / PKT on some variants (WCS_Armaments dep suggests at least one armed variant)
